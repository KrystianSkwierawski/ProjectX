# Dungeon runtime verification

Run after Unity import and Netcode IL processing with a licensed Unity 6000.1.15f1 Editor. Rebuild/restart the API and dedicated server first; use the normal LoadingScene client startup. Development API startup recreates/seeds its database. The generated-project builds and backend tests do not verify the following scenarios.

1. Confirm startup loads EnvironmentScene with NPC/crafting and hidden server-only spawners; TestScene/ServerScene no longer exist. In EnvironmentScene approach the violet portal at (-5, 2.984, -3). The cursor changes to the existing pointer within five units; hover does not change its translucent violet material. Beyond range, over UI and while typing, it must not activate. Left-click does nothing; right-click shows the existing loading view and enters the room.
2. Solo: verify floor collision, camera/movement, reachable Bean AI, combat/rewards and the exit portal. Inspect the loaded scene list: EnvironmentScene must be absent in the dungeon, and DungeonScene absent after returning; Main/UI/audio/LoadingScene stay loaded. In both directions verify that LoadingScene renders before travel and remains visible until the camera is positioned. Enter while walking/jumping, release controls during loading and stay idle for ten seconds: no horizontal drift or camera journey across the world. Repeat from a stationary position. Confirm both portals use the same translucent violet prefab and remain clickable from either side. Kill the Bean: it stays dead until departure. Exit, enter again: a new Bean exists. Also exit without killing it and verify a fresh run on return.
3. With three authenticated clients, form a party with two. Enter each separately; both see the same Bean and one another. The solo third client enters a separate room and sees neither party member. Combat, gathering references, trade invitations and kill rewards must not cross the boundary. Check a late connection while the party is already inside: it must load only EnvironmentScene initially and must not receive the server instance scene; NGO global scene synchronization is disabled.
4. One party member exits and re-enters while the other stays: preserve the run, including the dead Bean. Changing leader preserves the run. Leaving party returns only that member outside through loading; invite a new member and let them join via the portal. Test forming a party during a solo run and joining a party that already has a different run.
5. Disconnect in the dungeon and during loading, then reconnect: restore the persisted Arrival pose in front of the original world portal, never the offset room coordinates. Disconnect the last occupant: the next entrance gets a new room. Repeat rapid portal clicks and party changes while loading; duplicate/stale ready/arrival RPCs must not advance the transition twice.
6. Trading blocks voluntary portal travel. Leaving party during an uncommitted trade cancels that trade; an already accepted commit still settles through its server coordinator. Confirm no per-frame errors, leaked rooms/NavMeshes, stuck cursor/loading scopes or cross-instance objects after repeated runs.

Diagnostics: `Dungeon created/completed/released` and `Dungeon transition started/completed/timed out` include instance, client and transition identifiers. Portal rejections and failed persistence are logged. Check the normal ignored runtime logs under `Client/Logs/Runtime`.

After changing dungeon geometry, use `ProjectX > Bake Dungeon Navigation` and commit the prefab plus DungeonNavMesh.asset. Portal Destination selects the LocationEnum enum. There is no PortalId; requests without a matching portal in range must be rejected. Set the Arrival child on walkable ground outside the collider, with its blue Z axis pointing away from the portal. Check both position and facing, including after reconnect and forced party departure.

Template regression: enter the adjacent portal at (-1, 2.984, -3), verify only TemplateScene is loaded as the environment, no Bean/walls exist and the return portal brings the player in front of the matching world portal. Repeat with party members and a separate solo player. Verify a character standing in the spawn area does not cause the next entrant to spawn on their head. Each portal must reference its Arrival child; moving an unrelated collider must not change the chosen pose.
